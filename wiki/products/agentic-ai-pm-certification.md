# Agentic AI Product Management Certification (GenAI PM)

The flagship course from [[agentic-ai-institute]], taught by [[mahesh-yadav]] on Maven. Rated #1 on Maven with the most 5-star reviews among AI/PM certifications. Focused on agentic AI product mastery — architecture, evaluation, and deployment — as distinct from the interview-prep focus of the [[claude-bootcamp]].

## Curriculum

- **Foundation** — neural networks, deep learning vs. traditional ML, reinforcement learning, prompt engineering, agentic AI landscape
- **Business Translation** — RAG (and agentic RAG), AI feature prioritization, PRDs with token-cost and AI risk considerations
- **Evaluation & Responsible AI** — LLM-as-judge evaluators, responsible AI frameworks, guardrails, MCP integrations
- **Portfolio Building** — PRDs/case studies, KPI definition for AI agents, LoRA fine-tuning, small language models
- **Pitch & Launch** — demo hardening, evaluation dashboards, stakeholder presentations

## What's Included

Live sessions, lifetime access to recordings, peer community with lifetime post-cohort Fridays, 1-on-1 mentorship, dedicated engineer support, ~$1,380 in bonus platform credits, employer reimbursement templates, Maven satisfaction guarantee.
