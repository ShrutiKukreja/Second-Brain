# Agentic AI

The central subject of [[agentic-ai-institute]]'s entire content and course catalog: AI systems composed of autonomous agents that plan, use tools, and complete multi-step tasks — as opposed to single-shot generative AI. Framed throughout this corpus as the defining product-management skill shift of 2026.

## Why It Matters (per this corpus)

- "Why 2026 Is the Year of AI Builders (Agents, Orchestration & 15-Hour Tasks Explained)" (YouTube, 2026-04-21) frames long-running, orchestrated agent tasks as the new capability class PMs must design for.
- "AI Data Strategy for Product Managers: The Missing Skill in Agentic AI" (YouTube, 2026-05-31) treats data strategy as a prerequisite skill, not a nice-to-have.
- "The AI Stack Every AI PM Needs to Understand in 2026" (YouTube, 2026-03-23) positions agentic AI as requiring stack-level fluency (models, RAG, tools, evaluation), not just prompting.
