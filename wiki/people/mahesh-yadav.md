# Mahesh Yadav

Founder of the [[agentic-ai-institute]] and instructor of both Maven flagship courses in this knowledge base — the [[agentic-ai-pm-certification]] and the [[claude-bootcamp]]. He left a $1.3M compensation package at Google to build the Institute and now teaches agentic AI product management full-time, positioning himself as the "Super-IC PM" archetype he teaches in the bootcamp.

## Career

- Prior to founding the Institute, worked at Google in a role compensated at $1.3M — referenced directly in the YouTube video "Mahesh Left $1.3M at Google for This (The Future of PMs)" (published 2026-04-21).
- Cross-layer AI experience at major tech companies is credited (per course testimonials) with helping alumni break into AI roles.

## Teaching & Content

- Instructor for both Maven courses: **[[agentic-ai-pm-certification]]** ($3,000, ~18 hrs/week, 6-week cohorts) and **[[claude-bootcamp]]** ($2,999, ~6 hrs/week, interview-prep focus).
- Runs the [[agentic-ai-institute]] YouTube channel (233 videos, ~155K views, ~7.2K subscribers) and its LinkedIn company page (~8.5K followers).
- Publishes LinkedIn posts as "MAHESH YADAV" under the Agentic AI Institute company page — see [[linkedin-agentic-ai-institute]].
- Hosted a joint session with Gaurav Sen on technical PM careers ("From Task Execution to Problem Solving: Technical PMs & Engineers in 2026").
- Runs free public sessions, e.g. "Build and Ship with Claude Code" 3-part series and a Sunday masterclass, to funnel into paid cohorts.

## Key Ideas

- **Spec-based development**: "Vibe coding was the move in early 2025. Now it's table stakes... Spec-based development is the answer: PRD → Engineering Design → Spec → Code → Deploy." (LinkedIn, 2026-07-09) — see [[spec-based-development]].
- Emphasizes production-readiness over prototyping: candidates fail interviews for having "no monitoring, no guardrails, no deployment story" — see [[claude-code]].
- Views the current AI adoption gap (per an S&P Global report he cited: only 37% of enterprise AI initiatives are live and delivering value) as the biggest opportunity in tech right now.
